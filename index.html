      Notes & Definitions

      % Proportionate Achievement (used on both Circle and Division views)

      months_elapsed   = number of months in the selected period (e.g. Apr–Jun = 3)
pro_rata_target  = annual_revenue_target × (months_elapsed / 12)
actual_revenue   = sum of actual revenue earned in the selected period

% Proportionate Achievement = (actual_revenue / pro_rata_target) × 100

      Bands:  ≥ 100% — Strong (meeting or beating pro-rata) ·  80–99% — On track ·  60–79% — Needs attention ·  < 60% — At risk

      Why this metric: It tells a Divisional Head, at a glance, where the division stands against where it should be at this point in the financial year. 100% means the division is exactly on its annual pace.

      Caveats: Heavily weighted by POSB (≈ 66% of typical division target), so POSB front-loading can lift the early-FY number. RMS units appear low — they don't operate POSB / PLI / RPLI. Vijayawada Division can show a lower % despite high absolute revenue because its targets are also the largest.

      POSB Revenue Front-loading

      POSB revenue has three components, two of which are booked entirely in April:

      • Live Accounts × ₹219.23 per year → pro-rated monthly
      • Silent Accounts × ₹35.61 → booked entirely in April (not pro-rated)
      • NSC/KVP Certificates × ₹73.92 → booked when issued (continuous)

      This is why POSB shows much higher April attainment than other verticals. Not a data error.
